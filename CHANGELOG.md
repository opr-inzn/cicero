# Changelog

Each version's notes here become its release notes on the [releases page](https://github.com/opr-inzn/point/releases).

## 1.0

First release.

- Browse your whole font library in a grid or list, with adjustable size, weight, sample text and colors.
- Open a font for an editable preview, every style, a character grid, and an inspector with font info and OpenType features.
- Customize the sidebar: click **Edit** next to Library, or use Settings → Sidebar, to show or hide any item.
- Watch folders of fonts without installing them. Folders that only one app loads, like Adobe's Fonts folder, are marked for that app instead of showing as inactive.
- System Fonts item, with an option to keep the fonts that ship with macOS out of your other lists.
- Activate fonts by copying them to your Fonts folder or registering them in place. Deactivating never deletes your only copy.
- Right-click a font to reveal it in Finder, show its info, or file it under a style, collection or foundry.
